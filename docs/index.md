## Status

The plugins are under active development and target **Paper 26.x** on **Java 25**
(currently tested up to Paper 26.3, build 200). The Guard and Nexus source repositories
are private while the projects are in development; BlockProt Reloaded is public.
HollowKraft, the community server, is also in development and plans cross-play
between Java and Bedrock. This site is the public home for their documentation. Release
history and platform availability live on the [Releases](releases.md) page.
